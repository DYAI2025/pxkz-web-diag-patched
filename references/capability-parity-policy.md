# Capability Parity Policy

Core behavior is preserved only when the target runtime can:

1. access authorized public evidence;
2. distinguish measurements from observations;
3. validate JSON;
4. execute or faithfully reproduce the PDF renderer;
5. visually inspect or obtain human review of the PDF.

Missing tools are declared as runtime deviations. Adapters may change mechanics but may not add unsupported facts, remove evidence traceability, turn scenarios into forecasts, or bypass safety boundaries.
