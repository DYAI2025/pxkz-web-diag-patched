# Pixelkiez Decision-Visual Contract

A completed full customer report requires **four to seven decision-relevant visuals**, not charts for their own sake. The four core slots are overall/score state, category comparison, exactly four levers, and audit coverage; conditional slots require their own evidence.

Priority order:
1. direct measured charts tied to an important finding;
2. mandatory customer orientation visuals from the approved v2 catalog;
2. direct evidence comparison (`D01`) from two to four public evidence items supporting a selected finding;
3. other direct observed quantitative visuals;
4. score-derived V04/V07 orientation only as a fallback.

`D01` is not a thirteenth chart template. It is a non-chart evidence comparison card and does not alter the normative V01-V12 chart library.

Every customer visual must expose a plain-language measurement basis and `Beweist nicht` limitation. Raw internal grades, metric IDs, enum names, `DERIVED`, `SITE`, `NONE_OFFICIAL` and schema identifiers stay in machine-readable trace only.

A visual story fails when fewer than two truthful decision visuals can be produced. Never invent values, thresholds, cohorts, screenshots or comparison data merely to fill pages.
