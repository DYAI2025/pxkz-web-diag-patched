# Conditional 2-Year and 5-Year Scenarios

Future sections are scenarios, not forecasts.

## Required scenario set

For both two and five years, compare:

1. `inaction`: no planned upkeep beyond incident response;
2. `maintenance`: recurring updates, monitoring, content and small improvements;
3. `targeted_modernization`: bounded structural improvements plus maintenance;
4. `redesign`: evidence-led rebuild plus migration controls and maintenance.

## Construction

For every scenario state:

- assumptions;
- mechanism connecting current finding to possible future outcome;
- leading indicators that would support or falsify the scenario;
- confidence;
- known counterfactors;
- the first decision checkpoint.

Use phrases such as `if the current condition persists`, `could`, `is more exposed to`, and `requires monitoring`. Avoid `will`, guaranteed loss, fixed degradation rates, invented market growth, or unreviewed legal penalties.

Quantitative projections are allowed only when the customer supplies baseline data, model inputs are listed, sensitivity ranges are shown, and the output is labeled a scenario. Otherwise use qualitative risk direction.

## AI change

Treat AI-search systems, crawler controls, model behavior, and citation surfaces as dynamic. Evaluate durable foundations: crawlability, index eligibility, textual accessibility, clear entities, first-party evidence, structured data consistent with visible content, provenance, freshness, and measurement. Do not claim a special markup or fixed content chunk size guarantees AI visibility.

## Maintenance comparison

Maintenance reduces exposure only for items it actually covers. Name cadence by trigger rather than inventing universal frequency:

- after releases or dependency changes;
- after material content, offer, legal, or tracking changes;
- monthly or quarterly measurement review when data volume supports it;
- annual strategic review or earlier when a platform or business model changes.
